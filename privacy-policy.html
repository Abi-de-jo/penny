Penny Privacy Policy

Privacy & Data Protection Policy

Effective Date: October 23, 2026 | Application: Penny – Money Tracker
Executive Summary: Penny is a 100% local-first, offline financial ledger. The app does not request or possess the Android INTERNET permission. It is technically impossible for your financial data, SMS alerts, transaction notes, or personal information to be transmitted outside your device. Everything remains stored exclusively on your phone.

1. Introduction

Penny ("we", "our", or "the app") is designed with an inviolable commitment to user privacy. We believe financial tracking should be effortless without sacrificing personal confidentiality. This Privacy Policy details the types of information processed on-device and our strict technical safeguards.

2. Data Collection and Processing

Penny does not operate cloud servers, external databases, or user registration systems. We collect zero data from you.

  • No Cloud Accounts: You do not need to register, provide your email, phone number, or login credentials to use Penny.
  • Local Storage: All transactions, categories, titles, notes, and preferences are stored exclusively in an encrypted SQLite database on your device's internal storage sandbox.
  • No Network Transmission: Penny does not declare android.permission.INTERNET in its application manifest. The Android operating system physically forbids the app from making network calls or uploading data.

3. Permissions and Use Cases

Penny requests only the minimal permissions required to provide automated transaction tracking:

1. SMS Alert Access (RECEIVE_SMS)
  • Purpose: To automatically detect and record incoming transactional debit and credit receipts sent by your financial institutions.
  • Scope: Penny analyzes incoming SMS in real-time. It ignores non-financial messages, personal chats, and OTPs. Penny never scans, imports, or uploads your past inbox history.
2. Payment App Notification Listener (BIND_NOTIFICATION_LISTENER_SERVICE)
  • Purpose: When banks do not send carrier SMS for micro-transactions, Penny allows optional notification capture from verified payment apps (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, CRED) and banking apps.
  • Scope: Strict whitelisting is enforced. Personal messaging notifications (WhatsApp, Telegram, Signal, Contacts) are discarded immediately and never parsed or logged.
  • User Control: You can enable or disable this feature anytime in Penny Settings > Access or via Android Device Settings.
3. Biometric & Device Lock Authentication
  • Purpose: To prevent accidental or unauthorized deletion of financial records.
  • Operation: When swiping to delete a transaction, Penny uses Android's native BiometricPrompt (Fingerprint, Face unlock, or Lock Screen PIN). Biometric data is handled strictly by your device hardware keystore; Penny never has access to your biometric raw data.

4. Zero Bank Credentials

Penny is an observational tracking ledger. Penny will never ask for:

  • Your bank net banking passwords or usernames.
  • Your debit or credit card PIN, CVV, or expiry dates.
  • Your UPI MPIN or payment passwords.
  • One-Time Passwords (OTPs) — all OTP messages are detected and discarded immediately.

5. Third-Party Services and Analytics

Penny contains:

  • NO third-party advertising SDKs
  • NO behavioral tracking or analytics SDKs
  • NO cloud telemetry or crash-reporting servers

6. User Data Control and Deletion

You retain full authority over your data at all times:

  • Individual Record Deletion: Swipe right on any movement and authenticate with your phone PIN or fingerprint to delete it.
  • Complete Local Wipe: Navigate to Settings > Delete all Penny data to immediately purge all transactions, categories, and settings from local storage.
  • App Uninstall: Uninstalling Penny instantly removes the application sandbox and deletes all local database files.

7. Compliance with App Store Guidelines

Penny complies with the Google Play Developer Distribution Agreement, Google Play User Data and Financial Services policies, and the Indus Appstore Developer Guidelines. All sensitive data handling features provide prominent in-app disclosure and require explicit user consent.
